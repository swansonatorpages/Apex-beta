# Foundation beta — current product rules (October 2026)

This repository serves the standalone phased Foundation beta approved by the owner. The Foundation beta uses a separate localStorage key, `apex-foundation-beta-v1`.

- Start October 19, 2026, America/Boise; no scoring before start.
- Phase 1: morning light and Integrity Window. First week 4 of 5 Monday–Friday mornings; regular weeks 5 of 6 Sunday–Friday mornings.
- Outdoor light: 5-minute minimum, 10 encouraged. Desk light is an accepted fallback with device-specific usage guidance.
- One weekly Morning Light recovery: 10-minute outdoor walk plus plan next morning. Keep original history honest.
- Alignment Activity: Saturday, optional Sunday extension; declining it is valid.
- One Alignment Hold Reset remains recoverable through Friday with trigger and corrective action. Second interruption defers activity. Content Boundary Reset defers activity and restarts only Foundation counter. No next-week debt.
- Separate 90-day Foundation counter and permanent achievement; daily confirmed boundary records. Alignment Activity and Hold Reset do not reset it.
- Manual daily $1 Getaway Fund transfer confirmation. Money is never reset; transfer logging is unscored.
- Phase 2 automatically starts Sunday after the first successful week. In Bed target 11 PM; e-ink reading allowed. Two weekly 11:15 buffers, one excused night, plus one miss initially (5 of 6 nights).
- Sunday–Friday bedtimes are confirmed the next morning against the previous night.
- Ask before tightening bedtime after four weeks. Review before Week 3; no third habit automatically introduced.
- All visible language must remain discreet. No explicit terms in app labels, notifications, or history.
- iPhone Safari: safe-area insets, at least 16px input text, 48px touch targets, zoom remains enabled, relative subpath-compatible PWA URLs.
